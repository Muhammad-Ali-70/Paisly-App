# LinkedIn project (Profile → Add section → Projects)

**Title:**
Paisly – Expense & Udhar Book (Android App)

**Description:**
Paisly is a personal finance app on Google Play that helps people track daily expenses (kharcha), money lent and borrowed (udhar), and monthly budgets in one place. It supports cash, bank accounts and mobile wallets like JazzCash and Easypaisa.

I designed and built it end to end: the mobile app, the backend, and the marketing website. I took it from an unfinished side project to a production release.

Key features:
• Quick expense and income logging with categories, notes and filters
• Udhar book with due dates, partial payments and email reminders
• Monthly and per-category budgets with early warnings
• Multiple accounts with a total balance at a glance
• CSV export, light and dark mode

Tech: React Native, Zustand, MMKV, Node.js, Express, MongoDB, JWT
